**The best time for a safari in Zimbabwe is the dry season, from about June to October, and the peak for game viewing is August to October, when animals crowd around the last water.** June and July are cooler and best for walking safaris. The green season, from November to April, is hotter and wetter, with lush landscapes, migrant birds and lower lodge rates, but game is harder to find and Mana Pools and Gonarezhou close for part of it.

## Dry season versus green season

| Season                                         | Months            | Game viewing                                                                                           | Weather and comfort                                                          | Prices                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Early dry**                                  | May               | Good and improving. Grass is still long in places                                                      | Warm days, cool nights. Almost no rain                                       | Shoulder rates at some lodges |
| **Cool dry**                                   | June to July      | Very good. The bush thins and animals drink at permanent water                                         | Mild days, cold mornings (about 5°C in Hwange). Best for walking             | High season                   |
| **Late dry**                                   | August to October | The best. Animals gather at waterholes, pans and rivers                                                | Hot, reaching about 32–35°C in October in the low-lying parks                | High season                   |
| **Green season (early rains to end of rains)** | November to April | Harder. Water is everywhere, so animals spread out. Many antelope give birth, and migrant birds arrive | Hot and humid with afternoon storms. Heaviest rain from December to February | Lowest rates                  |

**What changes in the dry season:** grass dies back and many trees drop their leaves, so you can see further into the bush. Seasonal pans dry up, and animals have to come to permanent water, often to the pumped waterholes in Hwange or the rivers and floodplains of the Zambezi Valley and the lowveld.

**What changes in the green season:** the first storms usually arrive between November and early December. The bush turns green within days, and many animals give birth. Roads in remote parks get muddy or impassable, and some camps close.

## Best time by interest

- **Big game and predators:** August to October. See [where to see the Big Five](/wildlife/big-five-in-zimbabwe).
- **Painted dogs:** the denning season, roughly May to September. See [painted dogs](/wildlife/painted-dogs).
- **Walking safaris:** June to October, when the grass is short and visibility is good. See [walking and canoe safaris](/planning/walking-and-canoe-safaris).
- **Canoeing the Zambezi:** trips run most of the year, but June to October is best for wildlife.
- **Birding:** November to April, when migrant birds arrive and many residents breed. See [birding in Zimbabwe](/wildlife/birding-in-zimbabwe).
- **Photography:** the green season gives dramatic skies and green landscapes. The late dry season gives dust, golden light and animals at water.
- **Victoria Falls at full flow:** March to May. A safari in May can combine both. See [Victoria Falls](/destinations/victoria-falls).

## Prices and seasonality

**Park fees don't change with the seasons.** The ZimParks 2026 tariff sets one conservation fee for each park all year, such as US$24 a day for international visitors at Hwange and Mana Pools. See [park fees 2026](/essentials/park-fees).

**Lodge rates do.** Private camps usually charge the most from about June to October and the least in the green season. As an example, the 2026 published rates for one tented camp in Hwange run from **US$630** per person sharing per night in December to **US$1,390** from June to October, more than double. Rates and season dates vary by camp, so get quotes directly.

- **Book early for July to October.** Small camps and popular ZimParks sites, such as Mana Pools' Nyamepi riverfront and Chitake, fill up months ahead.
- **Shoulder months save money.** April, May and November often bring lower rates than the peak, but check that your camp is open.
- **Green season deals** suit repeat visitors, birders and photographers who don't mind heat and rain.
