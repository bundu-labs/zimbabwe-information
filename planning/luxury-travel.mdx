Zimbabwe has small, high-end safari camps in private concessions, a historic hotel at Victoria Falls and exclusive-use lodges in private reserves. Guest numbers are low, and service is personal.

**Written by [Bryan Fawcett](/about-this-guide).**

<Note>
  **Last reviewed: 25 October 2026** against the operators' own websites (Wilderness, African Bush Camps, Singita and The Victoria Falls Hotel), the US State Department's travel advisory and safari operators' published tipping guidance. We removed nightly rates, room counts and partner itinerary links we could not confirm. Luxury rates change every season, so ask the operator for current prices.
</Note>

## Luxury Safari Lodges & Camps

### Hwange National Park

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="star" title="Linkwasha Camp">
    **Wilderness camp in south-eastern Hwange**

    - Tented camp in a private concession on the edge of the Ngamo Plains
    - Exclusive traversing rights in the concession
    - Ask about night drives and walks
    - Rates: fully inclusive; ask Wilderness for current prices

    **Best For**: Couples seeking privacy and exceptional wildlife viewing

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="star" title="Little Makalolo">
    **Intimate Wilderness Experience**

    - Small Wilderness tented camp in a private concession in south-eastern Hwange
    - Overlooks a waterhole, with a hide beside it
    - Ask about family tents and minimum ages
    - Rates: fully inclusive; ask the operator for current prices

    **Best For**: Families and wildlife photography enthusiasts

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="star" title="Somalisa Camp">
    **Romance in the Wilderness**

    - African Bush Camps camp with seven tented suites
    - Overlooks a waterhole where elephants drink, close to camp
    - Swimming pool
    - Rates: fully inclusive; ask the operator for current prices

    **Best For**: Honeymooners and romantic getaways

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Mana Pools National Park

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="tree" title="Chikwenya">
    **Riverside Luxury**

    - Wilderness camp overlooking the Zambezi in the eastern section of Mana Pools
    - Known for walking safaris
    - Ask about canoeing and seasonal opening dates (Mana Pools is largely closed in the main rains)

    **Best For**: Adventure seekers and photography enthusiasts

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="tree" title="Little Ruckomechi">
    **Seasonal river camp**

    - Wilderness camp on the Zambezi in the west of Mana Pools, open in the dry season only
    - Views across the river to the Zambian escarpment
    - Ask about walking, game drives and boating

    **Best For**: Traditional safari experience with luxury touches

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Victoria Falls

<Card title="Victoria Falls Hotel" icon="hotel" color="#9C27B0">
  **The Grande Dame of Victoria Falls**

- Historic hotel, opened in 1904
- A short walk from the Falls, with views of the Victoria Falls Bridge
- Stanley's Terrace for high tea
- Ask the hotel for current room rates

**Perfect For**: History buffs and those wanting Falls convenience

</Card>

### Private & Bespoke Adventures

## When to Travel in Luxury

### Seasonal Considerations

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Peak Season">
    **June to October**

    Advantages:
    - Best wildlife viewing
    - Ideal weather conditions
    - All properties operational

    Considerations:
    - Highest rates
    - Book 6-12 months ahead
    - Some properties fully booked

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Green Season">
    **November to April**

    Advantages:
    - Lower green-season rates at many lodges
    - Lush landscapes
    - Excellent birding
    - Fewer tourists
    - Special offers common

    Considerations:
    - Some camps close
    - Occasional rain
    - Different wildlife patterns

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Shoulder Seasons">
    **May & November**

    Perfect Balance:
    - Good wildlife viewing
    - Pleasant weather
    - Better rates than peak
    - Excellent availability
    - Special packages offered

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Planning Your Luxury Safari

### Suggested luxury combinations

- **Victoria Falls and Hwange (6–7 days):** two or three nights at Victoria Falls, then four nights at a camp in a private Hwange concession.
- **Zambezi Valley (8–10 days):** Mana Pools for walking and canoeing, then Lake Kariba by houseboat or lodge. Best from May to October.
- **Zimbabwe in depth (12–14 days):** Victoria Falls, Hwange, Mana Pools, and Malilangwe or Gonarezhou in the south-east, linked by charter flights.

## Booking Luxury Travel

### What's Included in Luxury Rates

<Info>
Most luxury properties in Zimbabwe are fully inclusive:

- All meals and premium beverages
- Twice-daily game activities
- Park and conservation fees
- Laundry service
- Wi-Fi (where available)
- Some include emergency evacuation cover (check; it doesn't replace travel insurance)

**Usually Excluded**: Premium imports, spa treatments, shop purchases. Park fees are often but not always included; see [park fees](/essentials/park-fees).

</Info>

## Luxury Travel Tips

### Making the Most of Your Experience

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="lightbulb" title="Pre-Arrival Planning">

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="question" title="Is Zimbabwe safe for luxury travelers?">
    For most visitors, yes. The US rates Zimbabwe at level 2 of 4 ("exercise increased caution"), and most risks are petty crime in cities, road accidents and wildlife. Luxury lodges arrange transfers, which avoids most of these. See our [travel advice](/essentials/travel-advice).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="question" title="How far in advance should I book?">
    For peak season (June-October), book 6-12 months ahead. Green season offers more flexibility. Exclusive-use bookings need even longer lead times.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="question" title="What about internet connectivity?">
    Most luxury lodges offer Wi-Fi in main areas. Some provide in-room connectivity. Speeds vary but are generally sufficient for emails and messaging.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="question" title="Can special dietary needs be accommodated?">
    Usually, with advance notice. Vegan and gluten-free meals are routine at top lodges; ask about kosher or halal requirements well before you travel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
