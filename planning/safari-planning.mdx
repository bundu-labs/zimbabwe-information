**For a first Zimbabwe safari, combine Victoria Falls with Hwange National Park in the dry season, from about May to October.** Main Camp is about 195 km, roughly 2.5 hours, from Victoria Falls. For walking and canoeing, go to Mana Pools. For a lake safari by boat, go to Matusadona on Lake Kariba. For wild, empty country, go to Gonarezhou in the south-east. Park fees for international visitors are US$24 a day at Hwange, Mana Pools and Matusadona, and US$30 at Gonarezhou, in 2026.

### Popular combinations

- **First safari:** Victoria Falls and Hwange. Add [Zambezi National Park](/destinations/zambezi-national-park) for a short extra drive from town.
- **Zambezi Valley:** Mana Pools, Lake Kariba and Matusadona. Allow time: Harare to Kariba is about 365 km, and Kariba to Mana Pools is about 165 km via Makuti.
- **The south:** Gonarezhou with [Save Valley and Malilangwe](/destinations/save-valley-and-malilangwe) and [Great Zimbabwe](/destinations/great-zimbabwe).
- **History and wildlife:** Matobo, Bulawayo and Hwange.

See [travel distances](/essentials/distances) and our [itineraries](/planning/zimbabwe-itineraries).

## Step 2: pick the right time

| Season         | Months (roughly)  | What to expect                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dry season** | May to October    | The best game viewing, as animals gather at water. Cool nights in June and July. October is very hot           |
| **Rains**      | November to April | Green landscapes and migrant birds. Game spreads out, some roads get difficult, and some parks and camps close |

Closures to plan around:

- **Mana Pools** is largely closed in the main rains, roughly mid-November or December to March. ZimParks sets the dates each year.
- **Gonarezhou** is closed to all tourists from **10 January to 1 March**, and its remote camps close from late November.

See [best time for a safari](/wildlife/best-time-for-safari), [weather by month](/essentials/weather-by-month) and [when to visit](/when-to-visit).

## Step 3: choose your safari style

- **Lodge safari:** a private lodge or camp provides guided game drives, walks and meals. The easiest option, and often the best guiding. Lodges in private concessions can usually offer walks and night drives.
- **Self-drive:** hire a car (4x4 for the remote parks), stay in ZimParks chalets or campsites, and drive yourself. Cheapest and most independent, but you need to be self-sufficient.
- **Walking safari:** on foot with a licensed professional guide. Zimbabwean guides train for at least two years and pass a week-long practical exam. See [walking and canoe safaris](/planning/walking-and-canoe-safaris).
- **Canoe safari:** paddle the Zambezi below Kariba for 3 to 10 days, camping on islands.
- **Houseboat:** live aboard on [Lake Kariba](/destinations/lake-kariba), with tender boats for fishing and game viewing.
- **Fly-in:** charter flights link Victoria Falls, Hwange, Kariba, Mana Pools and other airstrips. Lodges or operators book them.

## Step 4: budget

Lodge rates change by season and operator, so get quotes directly. These are published 2026 prices you can plan around:

| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Hwange, Mana Pools or Matusadona conservation fee             | US$24 per person per day          | ZimParks tariff               |
| Gonarezhou conservation fee                                   | US$30 per day (US$20 overnight)   | ZimParks tariff               |
| Hwange camping or caravan site (up to 3 people)               | US$23 per site per night          | ZimParks tariff               |
| Hwange Main Camp chalet, one bedroom                          | US$60 per night                   | ZimParks tariff               |
| Mana Pools, Nyamepi riverfront campsite (up to 6 people)      | US$155 per site per night         | ZimParks tariff               |
| Gonarezhou developed campsite (e.g. Chipinda Pools)           | US$104 minimum per site per night | Gonarezhou Conservation Trust |
| Gonarezhou Bush Camps (Chilojo or Mahove), accommodation only | US$720 per person per night       | Gonarezhou Conservation Trust |
| Zambezi canoe safari, Kariba to Mana Pools, 6 days            | From about US$1,030 per person    | Natureways Safaris            |
| Kariba–Mlibizi car ferry, adult (one way, meals included)     | US$150                            | Kariba Ferries                |

**What lodge rates usually include:** accommodation, meals, guided activities and often park fees. Ask about park fees, drinks, transfers and charter flights, which are often extra.

**Other costs:** a visa (US$30 single entry for most nationalities; see [visas](/essentials/visas-and-entry)), travel insurance with medical evacuation, and tips. See our [tipping guide](/essentials/tipping-guide).

## Step 5: book

- **ZimParks camps, chalets and lodges** (Hwange, Mana Pools, Matobo and others): book through ZimParks at info@zimparks.org.zw or +263 242 707625. Popular sites such as Mana Pools' Nyamepi riverfront and Chitake fill months ahead in the dry season.
- **Gonarezhou campsites and bush camps:** book through the Gonarezhou Conservation Trust at reservations@gonarezhou.org or +263 779 788 811.
- **Private lodges:** book directly or through a tour operator. For July to October, book early, as small camps fill up.
- **Ask your guide for their licence.** Zimbabwean professional guides are licensed by ZimParks, and a reputable operator will show it.

## Step 6: get ready

- **Malaria:** most safari areas are in malaria zones. See a travel health clinic before you go. See [health and safety](/essentials/health-and-safety).
- **Insurance:** get [travel insurance](/essentials/travel-insurance) that covers medical evacuation, and walking or canoeing if you plan to do them.
- **Cash:** bring US dollars in small notes for park fees and tips. Card machines at remote gates don't always work. See [currency and money](/essentials/currency-and-money).
- **Packing:** neutral colours, warm layers for June and July mornings, a hat, sunscreen, binoculars and a torch. Light aircraft often limit luggage, so ask your operator about soft bags and weight limits. See the [packing guide](/essentials/packing-guide).
- **Etiquette:** stay in the vehicle unless your guide says otherwise, keep quiet near animals, and never feed wildlife. See [safari etiquette](/essentials/safari-etiquette).

## Safaris with children

- **Canoe safaris:** the minimum age is usually 14 on scheduled trips. Some operators run private family trips for younger children.
- **Walking:** age limits are set by each operator. Singita's bush walks at Malilangwe, for example, have a minimum age of 16.
- **Easy options:** Hwange's ZimParks camps, Matobo and Zambezi National Park are easy to reach and good for families.
