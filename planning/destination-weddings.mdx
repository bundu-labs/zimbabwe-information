## Legal Requirements

### Getting Legally Married in Zimbabwe

Civil marriages are governed by the Marriages Act [Chapter 5:17], in force since 16 September 2022. The key rules for visitors:

<Card title="Legal Requirements" icon="file-contract">

**Who can marry:**

- Both parties must be at least 18. No one under 18 may marry in Zimbabwe
- Neither party may already be married
- The constitution prohibits marriage between people of the same sex (section 78(3)), so same-sex couples can't marry in Zimbabwe, though a symbolic ceremony is possible
- You must both be present: marriage by proxy is not allowed

**Before the wedding, you need one of these:**

1. **Banns** published at a church on three successive Sundays (or the main weekly service day) before the wedding
2. **A notice of intention to marry** posted by a magistrate for 15 days. The applicant must have lived in that magistrate's district for at least 14 days first, so this rarely suits short-stay visitors
3. **A marriage licence from a magistrate**, without banns or notice. You both apply in person, make a solemn declaration that there is no lawful impediment, and pay the prescribed fee. The magistrate can question you and ask for documents. This is the usual route for couples who fly in

Banns or a notice published in your home country count if you can prove they were done properly under that country's law. Banns, notices and licences lapse if you don't marry within three months.

**The ceremony:**

- It must be performed by a **marriage officer** (for example a magistrate or a minister of religion designated as a marriage officer)
- It can take place in a church, a public office, a private house **or another place the marriage officer approves**, so a legal ceremony at a lodge or garden venue is possible if your marriage officer agrees
- At least **two witnesses aged 18 or over** must be present

**Documents to have ready** (the magistrate decides exactly what to ask for):

- Valid passports
- Birth certificates
- Proof that you're free to marry, such as a certificate of no impediment or a sworn statement of single status from your home country
- If divorced: the final divorce decree. If widowed: the death certificate
- Certified English translations of any documents in another language

**If one partner is Zimbabwean:** extra steps apply, which law firms list as including clearance from the Department of Immigration and police clearances. Take advice from a Zimbabwean lawyer or the Registrar General's office.

**Recognition at home:** check with your home country's marriage registry whether it recognises a Zimbabwean marriage certificate as it stands, or whether it needs to be legalised or registered.

**Alternative Approach:**
Many couples marry legally at home and have a blessing ceremony in Zimbabwe. This avoids the paperwork and timing risk, and you still get your Zimbabwe wedding.

</Card>

### Safari Venues

<Card title="Safari Wedding Venues" icon="tent">

Safari camps are small, so most suit weddings of about 20 guests or fewer, often on an exclusive-use basis. Ask each camp about capacity and whether it hosts weddings.

| Area            | Camps to ask                   | Style                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Hwange**      | Somalisa, Linkwasha, The Hide  | Tented camps overlooking waterholes         |
| **Mana Pools**  | Ruckomechi, Kanga Camp         | Riverside or inland camps (dry season only) |
| **Lake Kariba** | Bumi Hills, private houseboats | Lakeside lodge or a boat to yourselves      |

**Safari Wedding Benefits:**

- Exclusive use of camp possible
- Wildlife as wedding guests
- Bush dinner receptions under stars
- Completely unique experience
- Built-in honeymoon location

</Card>

## Wedding Planning

### Working with Planners

<Card title="Wedding Planners" icon="list-check">

**Why Use a Local Planner:**

- Navigate Zimbabwe's legal requirements
- Relationships with venues and vendors
- Handle logistics you can't manage remotely
- Crisis management on the day
- Cultural knowledge
- Local vendor recommendations

**What Planners Handle:**

- Documentation and legal process
- Venue booking and coordination
- Catering arrangements
- Décor and flowers
- Photography and videography
- Music and entertainment
- Guest accommodation
- Transport logistics
- Day-of coordination

**Finding a Planner:**

- Ask venues for recommendations
- Check reviews and portfolios
- Interview via video call
- Clarify pricing (package vs. hourly)
- Confirm they've done destination weddings before

</Card>

### Timeline

<Card title="Planning Timeline" icon="calendar">

**12+ Months Before:**

- Choose date and venue
- Hire wedding planner
- Book accommodation for guests
- Send save-the-dates
- Start document preparation

**6-9 Months Before:**

- Finalize guest list
- Book vendors (photographer, florist, music)
- Plan ceremony details
- Arrange guest activities
- Book flights

**3-6 Months Before:**

- Send formal invitations
- Finalize menu
- Confirm all bookings
- Plan rehearsal dinner
- Arrange transport for guests

**1-3 Months Before:**

- Final dress fittings
- Confirm all vendors
- Submit legal documents
- Create day-of timeline
- Brief wedding party

**1-2 Weeks Before:**

- Arrive in Zimbabwe
- Final venue walk-through
- Complete legal ceremony (if doing here)
- Rehearsal
- Enjoy!

</Card>

## Photography

<Card title="Wedding Photography" icon="camera">

**Zimbabwe Wedding Photo Opportunities:**

- Victoria Falls viewing points (spray adds drama!)
- Victoria Falls Bridge
- Zambezi River at sunset
- Safari settings with wildlife
- Lodge gardens and architecture
- Helicopter aerial shots
- Gorge and canyon views

**Hiring a Photographer:**

- Local photographers know best spots and light
- Book 6+ months ahead for peak season
- Ask for full wedding portfolios
- Discuss travel if multi-location
- Consider engagement shoot day before

**Tips for Great Photos:**

- Morning light best (5:30-8am)
- Golden hour stunning (4:30-6pm)
- Falls spray can affect equipment—discuss with photographer
- White dress + falls spray = potential see-through (consider dress fabric)
- Safari venues—neutral colors for guests

**Drone Photography:**

- Drones must be registered and cleared with the Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe
- Parks and many lodges don't allow drones without specific permission
- Photographer should arrange permissions

</Card>

## Ceremony Options

### Types of Ceremonies

<Card title="Ceremony Styles" icon="heart">

**Legal Civil Ceremony:**

- Performed by a marriage officer, at a public office or another place the marriage officer approves
- Brief, formal
- Handles legal requirements
- Can be done before the "main" ceremony, or as part of it if your marriage officer agrees
- Two witnesses aged 18 or over required

**Religious Ceremony:**

- Bring your own officiant or find local
- Churches available in Victoria Falls
- Most faiths can be accommodated
- May require additional planning

**Symbolic/Blessing Ceremony:**

- At your chosen venue
- Performed by celebrant of choice
- Fully customizable
- Not legally binding unless a marriage officer solemnises it with the legal requirements met
- Most popular for destination weddings

**Traditional African Elements:**

- Drumming and dancing
- Traditional dress elements
- African blessings
- Cultural rituals
- Can blend with Western ceremony

</Card>

## Practical Matters

### Best Time for Weddings

<Card title="Timing Your Wedding" icon="sun">

| Season          | Pros                                                     | Cons                                  |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **April-May**   | Pleasant weather, falls near peak flow, green landscapes | Heavy spray at the falls              |
| **June-August** | Dry, comfortable, good wildlife                          | Peak season, book early               |
| **September**   | Excellent weather, good wildlife                         | Getting hot                           |
| **October**     | Good wildlife                                            | Very hot, falls at low water          |
| **November**    | Green season starting, good rates                        | Possible storms                       |
| **Dec-March**   | Lowest prices                                            | Hot, rainy; Mana Pools largely closed |

**Best Months:** April-June or September
**Avoid:** Late October (extreme heat), January-February (heavy rain)

</Card>
