**For a first trip to Zimbabwe, spend 3 nights at Victoria Falls and 4 nights in Hwange National Park.** With 10 days, add Mana Pools or Matusadona on the Zambezi. With 14 days, drive the full circuit through Matobo, Great Zimbabwe and the Eastern Highlands or Gonarezhou. Below are five night-by-night plans, with how to get between stops, what to do each day, the best months, a daily cost per person and the park fees to budget.

**Written by [Bryan Fawcett](/about-this-guide).**

  **Last reviewed: 21 October 2026.** Park fees are the official 2026 ZimParks international rates from our [park fees guide](/essentials/park-fees). Distances and driving times are approximate. Check prices and border rules before you book.
</Note>

### How to read the costs

The daily cost bands match our [introduction](/introduction): **budget** US$50–100, **mid-range** US$150–350, **safari lodge** US$400–900 and **luxury** US$1,000+ per person per day. They cover accommodation, food and transport inside Zimbabwe. International flights, visas, park fees, big activities (rafting, helicopter flights) and tips are extra.

Park fees below are per person at the **international** rate. SADC and Zimbabwean visitors pay less. Many lodges inside parks include the fees in their rates, so ask before you pay twice.

## 8 days: Victoria Falls and Hwange

**Seven nights: 3 at Victoria Falls, then 4 in Hwange National Park.** This is the classic first trip. You see the falls at your own pace, then get four full days of game viewing in Zimbabwe's largest park.

| Night | Where you sleep      | Getting there                                                     |
| ----- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–3   | Victoria Falls town  | Fly into Victoria Falls Airport, 20–30 minutes from town          |
| 4–7   | Hwange National Park | Road transfer, about 195 km and 2.5–3 hours to Main Camp          |
| Day 8 | Fly home             | Road transfer back to Victoria Falls Airport, or a charter flight |

### Day by day

- **Day 1: arrive at Victoria Falls.** Check in, then take a sunset cruise on the Zambezi above the falls.
- **Day 2: the falls.** Walk the rainforest trail in the morning (allow 2–3 hours). In the afternoon, take a helicopter flight or walk onto the Victoria Falls Bridge. See [Zimbabwe side vs Zambia side](/destinations/victoria-falls-zimbabwe-vs-zambia) if you want to cross.
- **Day 3: your choice.** White-water rafting, a game drive in the Zambezi National Park, 10 minutes from town, or a [Chobe day trip](#victoria-falls-and-chobe-botswana) into Botswana.
- **Day 4: transfer to Hwange.** Leave after breakfast and arrive for lunch. Afternoon game drive.
- **Days 5–7: safari.** Morning and afternoon game drives, a guided walk, and time at the waterholes and hides. Private concessions also offer night drives.
- **Day 8: depart.** Drive back to Victoria Falls Airport, or take a 30-minute charter flight.

### Getting around

- **Victoria Falls to Hwange:** private road transfers cost about US$50–80 per person, according to our [transport guide](/essentials/transportation). Charter flights take about 30 minutes.
- **In Hwange:** most lodges include game drives. Self-drivers need a high-clearance vehicle or a 4x4 inside the park.

### Best months

**June to October.** The bush is dry, and animals gather at the waterholes, with the best game viewing from August to October. The falls are fullest from about February to May. June and July are a good compromise, with plenty of water at the falls and good game viewing.

### Cost

- **Mid-range (US$150–350 a day):** a guesthouse or hotel in Victoria Falls town, and ZimParks lodges or a mid-range lodge near Hwange Main Camp.
- **Safari lodge (US$400–900 a day):** a tented camp in a private concession in Hwange, with all game drives included.

### Park fees to budget

| Fee                                         | US$ per person    |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Victoria Falls Rainforest, one visit        | 58                |
| Hwange, staying overnight: 5 days at US$12  | 60                |
| Zambezi National Park, day visit (optional) | 18                |
| **Total**                                   | **about 118–136** |

## 10 days: Victoria Falls, Hwange and Mana Pools

**Nine nights: 3 at Victoria Falls, 3 in Hwange and 3 in Mana Pools.** You add the Zambezi Valley, where you can canoe past elephants and walk with a professional guide. Swap Mana Pools for **Matusadona** on Lake Kariba if you prefer boat-based game viewing.

| Night  | Where you sleep            | Getting there                                                   |
| ------ | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–3    | Victoria Falls town        | Fly into Victoria Falls Airport                                 |
| 4–6    | Hwange National Park       | Road transfer, about 195 km and 2.5–3 hours, or a short charter |
| 7–9    | Mana Pools (or Matusadona) | Light-aircraft charter to the park's airstrip                   |
| Day 10 | Fly home                   | Charter flight to Harare for your international flight          |

### Day by day

- **Days 1–3: Victoria Falls.** As in the 8-day plan: a sunset cruise, the rainforest walk and one big activity.
- **Day 4: to Hwange.** Afternoon game drive.
- **Days 5–6: Hwange.** Game drives, a guided walk and time at the waterholes.
- **Day 7: fly to Mana Pools.** Afternoon game drive or walk along the river.
- **Days 8–9: Mana Pools.** Canoe on the Zambezi, walk with a guide between the pools, and watch elephants feed on the floodplain.
- **Day 10: depart.** Fly to Harare to connect with your flight home.

**The Matusadona option.** Fly to Kariba and take a boat across the lake, or fly to the Matusadona airstrip. Days are spent on boat safaris along the shoreline, game drives and walks. Read our [Matusadona guide](/destinations/matusadona-national-park).

### Getting around

- **Charter flights** are the practical way to link Hwange and the Zambezi Valley. Your safari operator books them. Our [distances guide](/essentials/distances) gives about 2 hours for a charter from Victoria Falls to Mana Pools.
- **By road**, Mana Pools is about 390 km (about 7 hours) from Harare, the last stretch on rough gravel. A 4x4 is essential.

### Best months

**June to October.** Most Mana Pools camps open only from about May to November, and the dry season brings animals to the river. September and October are very hot in the valley, up to 33–35°C.

### Cost

**Safari lodge (US$400–900 a day), or more.** Charter flights and remote camps put this trip at the top of the safari lodge band. Some Mana Pools camps are in the luxury band (US$1,000+).

### Park fees to budget

| Fee                                                          | US$ per person |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------- |
| Victoria Falls Rainforest, one visit                         | 58             |
| Hwange, staying overnight: 4 days at US$12                   | 48             |
| Mana Pools or Matusadona, staying overnight: 4 days at US$24 | 96             |
| **Total**                                                    | **about 202**  |

## 14 days: the full circuit

**Thirteen nights covering the falls, the parks and Zimbabwe's history.** You go from Victoria Falls and Hwange to the granite hills of Matobo, the stone city of Great Zimbabwe, and then either the cool Eastern Highlands or the remote Gonarezhou.

| Night  | Where you sleep                   | Getting there                                                                                                                   |
| ------ | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–3    | Victoria Falls town               | Fly into Victoria Falls Airport                                                                                                 |
| 4–6    | Hwange National Park              | About 195 km, 2.5–3 hours                                                                                                       |
| 7–8    | Matobo Hills (or Bulawayo)        | About 325–340 km via Bulawayo, 4.5–5.5 hours                                                                                    |
| 9–10   | Great Zimbabwe or Lake Mutirikwi  | About 350 km via Bulawayo and Masvingo, 4.5–5.5 hours                                                                           |
| 11–13  | **Option A:** Bvumba, near Mutare | About 345 km via Masvingo and Mutare, 5–6 hours                                                                                 |
| 11–13  | **Option B:** Gonarezhou          | About 250 km via Chiredzi, about 4 hours                                                                                        |
| Day 14 | Fly home from Harare              | From Bvumba about 290 km, 3.5–4.5 hours. From Gonarezhou about 550 km, 7–8 hours, or a charter from Buffalo Range near Chiredzi |

### Day by day

- **Days 1–3: Victoria Falls.** The rainforest walk, a sunset cruise and one big activity.
- **Days 4–6: Hwange.** Game drives and a guided walk.
- **Day 7: to Matobo.** Drive via Bulawayo and arrive in the hills in the afternoon.
- **Day 8: Matobo.** Track white rhino on foot with a ranger, see San rock art and climb to World's View, Cecil Rhodes's grave. See our [Matobo Hills guide](/destinations/matobo-hills).
- **Day 9: to Great Zimbabwe.** A long drive. Stop in Bulawayo for lunch or the Natural History Museum.
- **Day 10: Great Zimbabwe.** Spend the morning at the Hill Complex and the Great Enclosure, then relax by Lake Mutirikwi. See our [Great Zimbabwe guide](/destinations/great-zimbabwe).
- **Days 11–13, option A: the Eastern Highlands.** Forest walks, tea and coffee estates, and views over Mozambique from the Bvumba. If you have an extra night, add Chimanimani, about 150 km from Mutare, for mountain hiking. See our [Eastern Highlands guide](/destinations/eastern-highlands).
- **Days 11–13, option B: Gonarezhou.** The red Chilojo Cliffs, big elephant herds and almost no other visitors. Self-drivers need a 4x4 and full fuel tanks. See our [Gonarezhou guide](/destinations/gonarezhou).
- **Day 14: to Harare.** Drive to Harare for your flight home.

### Getting around

- **Self-drive or a driver-guide.** This route is mostly on tar roads, apart from park roads and the last stretch into Gonarezhou. Hire a high-clearance vehicle or a 4x4. Many visitors hire a local driver-guide instead.
- **Don't drive at night.** Unlit vehicles, people and animals make night driving dangerous. Plan each leg to arrive before dark.

### Best months

**May to October.** The dry season gives good game viewing in Hwange and Gonarezhou and clear walking weather at Matobo and Great Zimbabwe. Pack warm layers for June and July nights, and expect mist in the Eastern Highlands.

### Cost

**Mostly mid-range (US$150–350 a day)** with lodges, guesthouses and car hire or a driver. Expect to pay more for nights at a safari lodge in Hwange (US$400–900).

### Park fees to budget

| Fee                                                            | US$ per person                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Victoria Falls Rainforest, one visit                           | 58                                         |
| Hwange, staying overnight: 4 days at US$12                     | 48                                         |
| Matobo, 2 day visits at US$18 (US$10 a day if you stay inside) | 36                                         |
| Great Zimbabwe entry, set by National Museums and Monuments    | about 15                                   |
| Option B only: Gonarezhou, staying overnight, 4 days at US$20  | 80                                         |
| **Total**                                                      | **about 157 (option A) or 237 (option B)** |

If you drive yourself, add vehicle fees. A Zimbabwe-registered hire car pays US$5 a day on a day visit, or US$2 a day when you stay inside Hwange, Matobo or Gonarezhou. See [park fees](/essentials/park-fees).

## Victoria Falls and Chobe (Botswana)

**Five or six nights: 3 at Victoria Falls, then 2 or 3 in Kasane on the Chobe River in Botswana.** Chobe National Park is known for huge elephant herds along the river, and it's only about 80 km from Victoria Falls.

| Night      | Where you sleep            | Getting there                                                                                                |
| ---------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1–3        | Victoria Falls town        | Fly into Victoria Falls Airport                                                                              |
| 4–5 or 4–6 | Kasane, on the Chobe River | Road transfer, about 70 km to the Kazungula border, then a few km to Kasane. About 1.5 hours with the border |
| Last day   | Fly home                   | Fly out of Kasane, or drive back to Victoria Falls Airport                                                   |

### Day by day

- **Days 1–3: Victoria Falls.** The rainforest walk, a sunset cruise and one big activity. With a KAZA UniVisa you can also cross to the Zambia side.
- **Day 4: to Kasane.** Cross at Kazungula in the morning. Afternoon boat cruise on the Chobe River.
- **Days 5–6: Chobe.** An early game drive along the riverfront, then another boat cruise in the afternoon, when elephants come down to drink.
- **Last day: depart.** Fly out of Kasane, or drive back to Victoria Falls for your flight.

### Visas and the border

- **Kazungula border** (Zimbabwe–Botswana) is open from 6am to 6pm. Arrive early to avoid queues.
- **KAZA UniVisa (US$50):** covers Zimbabwe and Zambia for up to 30 days, plus **day trips** to Botswana through Kazungula. See [visas and entry](/essentials/visas-and-entry).
- **Sleeping in Botswana:** the UniVisa is described as covering day trips to Botswana. If you spend nights in Kasane and then come back into Zimbabwe, check that your visa lets you re-enter. You may need a double-entry visa (US$70 for British citizens) or a new single-entry visa (US$30 for most nationalities) at the border. The simplest plan is to **end your trip in Kasane**.
- **Botswana entry:** many nationalities, including UK, US and EU citizens, don't need a visa for Botswana. Check your own. Travel sites report that Botswana charges non-SADC visitors a US$30 Tourism Development Levy on arrival.

### Best months

**May to October.** In the dry season, elephants and buffalo come to the Chobe River in large numbers. For the falls at full flow, go from about February to May, but game viewing in Chobe is better later in the year.

### Cost

- **Mid-range (US$150–350 a day):** a guesthouse in Victoria Falls, and a Kasane lodge where you book boat cruises and game drives separately.
- **Safari lodge (US$400–900 a day):** a Chobe riverfront lodge with activities included.

### Park fees to budget

| Fee                                              | US$ per person                             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Victoria Falls Rainforest, one visit             | 58                                         |
| Chobe National Park, Botswana                    | Check. Usually included in activity prices |
| Botswana Tourism Development Levy, if it applies | 30                                         |

Chobe park fees are set by Botswana's Department of Wildlife and National Parks, not ZimParks. Kasane lodges and boat operators usually include them. We couldn't confirm the 2026 rates, so ask when you book.

## 10-day budget self-drive road trip

**Nine nights on a road trip from Victoria Falls to Harare, sleeping in ZimParks camps, campsites and guesthouses.** This is the cheapest way to see the big highlights. You drive yourself and pay park fees directly.

| Night  | Where you sleep                                | Getting there                                             |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–2    | Victoria Falls town (backpackers or camping)   | Fly into Victoria Falls Airport and collect your hire car |
| 3–4    | Hwange Main Camp (ZimParks camping or chalets) | About 195 km, 2.5–3 hours                                 |
| 5      | Bulawayo                                       | About 290–305 km, about 4 hours                           |
| 6–7    | Matobo, Maleme camp (ZimParks)                 | About 35 km, 30–45 minutes                                |
| 8–9    | Great Zimbabwe or Masvingo                     | About 350 km via Bulawayo and Masvingo, 4.5–5.5 hours     |
| Day 10 | Drop the car in Harare and fly home            | About 320 km, about 4 hours                               |

### Day by day

- **Day 1: arrive.** Collect your car, buy supplies and walk into town.
- **Day 2: the falls.** Walk the rainforest trail, then walk onto the Victoria Falls Bridge. Take your passport.
- **Day 3: to Hwange.** Drive the tar road to Main Camp and do a late-afternoon drive to the nearby waterholes.
- **Day 4: Hwange.** Drive yourself between waterholes, or join a ZimParks guided drive or walk.
- **Day 5: to Bulawayo.** Explore the city centre and the Natural History Museum.
- **Days 6–7: Matobo.** Rock art, World's View and walks among the granite hills. Book rhino tracking with a ranger.
- **Day 8: to Great Zimbabwe.** A long driving day. Arrive by mid-afternoon.
- **Day 9: Great Zimbabwe.** Explore the ruins in the cool of the morning.
- **Day 10: to Harare.** Drop off the car and catch your flight.

### Getting around

- **Car hire:** a basic car costs about US$50–80 a day and an SUV US$80–120, according to our [transport guide](/essentials/transportation). One-way hire costs extra. A high-clearance car is best for Hwange's park roads.
- **Fuel:** fill up in every town. Stations can be more than 100 km apart. Pay in US dollars cash.
- **Daylight only:** don't drive after dark.
- **Without a car:** buses link Victoria Falls, Bulawayo, Masvingo and Harare, but you will need a tour or transfer to reach Hwange and Matobo.

### Best months

**May to October.** The roads are dry and the parks are at their best. From November to March, rain can slow you down. Allow 20–50% more time on rural roads.

### Cost

**US$75–150 per person per day** for two people sharing a hired car. That is the top of the budget band (US$50–100) to the bottom of mid-range (US$150–350), because car hire adds to the cost. Camping keeps it lower, and guesthouses and chalets push it higher.

### Park fees to budget

| Fee                                                              | US$                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Victoria Falls Rainforest, one visit, per person                 | 58                                                 |
| Hwange, staying overnight: 3 days at US$12, per person           | 36                                                 |
| Matobo, staying overnight at Maleme: 3 days at US$10, per person | 30                                                 |
| Great Zimbabwe entry, per person                                 | about 15                                           |
| **Total per person**                                             | **about 139**                                      |
| Hwange camping site, up to 3 people, per night                   | 23                                                 |
| Maleme camping, per person, per night                            | 12                                                 |
| Vehicle fees, Zimbabwe-registered car, Hwange and Matobo         | US$2 a day staying overnight, US$5 for a day visit |
