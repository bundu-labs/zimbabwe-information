Masvingo is the capital of Masvingo Province in south-eastern Zimbabwe and the gateway to the country's most important historical site, the Great Zimbabwe ruins. This provincial city has affordable places to stay, and easy access to Lake Mutirikwi, its recreational park and, further south-east, Gonarezhou National Park. For travellers interested in Zimbabwe's history, it makes a practical base.

**Written by [Bryan Fawcett](/about-this-guide).**

## Top attractions

### Great Zimbabwe (about 27 km)

<Card title="UNESCO World Heritage site" icon="monument">
  The main reason to visit Masvingo, and one of Africa's most important archaeological sites.

**About Great Zimbabwe:**

- The largest ancient stone construction in sub-Saharan Africa
- Built and occupied between the 11th and 15th centuries
- Capital of a kingdom that traded as far as the East African coast
- Gave Zimbabwe its name ("houses of stone")
- Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1986

**Key features:**

- **Hill Complex:** the oldest part, on the hilltop
- **Great Enclosure:** curved granite walls up to about 11 m high
- **Valley Ruins:** where most people lived
- **Zimbabwe Birds:** soapstone carvings, now the national emblem

**Visiting:**

- Entry fee: set by National Museums and Monuments of Zimbabwe (NMMZ). NMMZ lists site fees of up to US$15 but not the current Great Zimbabwe rate, so check at the gate
- NMMZ lists opening hours of 8:00 to 16:30 daily
- Allow three to four hours
- Guides are available at the entrance (ask for the current rate)
- Go early in the morning or late in the afternoon to avoid the midday heat

**From Masvingo:**

- About 27 km south-east of the town, around 30 to 40 minutes' drive on a tarred road
- Well signposted

</Card>

### Lake Mutirikwi (formerly Lake Kyle)

<Card title="Lake and recreational park" icon="sailboat">
  A large reservoir south-east of Masvingo, close to Great Zimbabwe. It was built to supply irrigation water to the lowveld sugar estates.

**Activities:**

- Fishing, including bass and bream
- Boating
- Scenic drives and birdwatching
- Picnics

**Access:**

- Several roads lead to the lake shore
- The recreational park on the lake is run by ZimParks and charges conservation fees (see below)

</Card>

---

### Lake Mutirikwi Recreational Park (Kyle)

<Card title="Wildlife and recreation" icon="binoculars">
  A ZimParks recreational park on the shores of Lake Mutirikwi, about 30 km from Masvingo.

**Wildlife:**

- White rhino
- Giraffe, zebra, buffalo and antelope such as kudu, eland and waterbuck
- Hippo and crocodile in the lake
- Good birding, including fish eagles

**Activities:**

- Game drives
- Fishing (permit required)
- Picnics

**2026 ZimParks conservation fees (per person per day):**

| Visitor       | Day visit | Overnight |
| ------------- | --------- | --------- |
| International | US$12     | US$5      |
| SADC          | US$10     | US$4      |
| Zimbabwean    | US$3      | US$2      |

ZimParks has lodges and campsites in the park. Book through [ZimParks](https://www.zimparks.org.zw).

</Card>

---

### Mushandike Sanctuary

<Card title="Quieter game viewing" icon="tent">
  A smaller ZimParks sanctuary about 30 to 40 km west of Masvingo.

**About:**

- Smaller and quieter than the Lake Mutirikwi park
- Plains game and good birding
- Self-drive game viewing

**2026 ZimParks conservation fees (per person per day):** US$12 international, US$10 SADC, US$3 Zimbabwean for a day visit. ZimParks also lists hostel beds here.

</Card>

### Masvingo town

<Card title="Provincial capital" icon="building">
  Masvingo town is a practical stop with everyday Zimbabwean life.

**Practical benefits:**

- Supermarkets for supplies
- Banks and ATMs
- Fuel stations
- Medical facilities

**Local life:**

- Home to Great Zimbabwe University
- Local markets selling produce and crafts

**Shopping:**
Soapstone carvings are made locally. Buy direct from carvers near Great Zimbabwe and on the roads around Masvingo.

</Card>

## Masvingo as a gateway

### To Gonarezhou National Park

<Card>
Masvingo is a common starting point for trips to Gonarezhou:

**Route:** Masvingo to Chiredzi (about 200 km), then on to Chipinda Pools
**Distance:** about 260 km to Chipinda Pools
**Time:** allow most of a day

**Why go via Masvingo:**

- Stock up on supplies
- Fill up with fuel
- Combine with Great Zimbabwe

**Tip:** Consider a night in Masvingo before the long drive to Gonarezhou.

</Card>

## Practical information

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="bed" title="Accommodation">
    **In Masvingo town:** hotels, guesthouses and self-catering apartments.

    **Near Great Zimbabwe:** several hotels and lodges, and camping. See accommodation in our [Great Zimbabwe guide](/destinations/great-zimbabwe).

    **Lake Mutirikwi:** ZimParks lodges and campsites in the recreational park, and private lodges around the lake.

    Compare current rates and reviews on booking sites. For ZimParks accommodation, book through ZimParks.

    **Budget tip:** stay in Masvingo town and make a day trip to Great Zimbabwe.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="utensils" title="Food and dining">
    - Hotel restaurants and local eateries in town
    - Fast-food outlets and supermarkets for self-catering
    - Limited options at Great Zimbabwe itself, so bring snacks and water

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="car" title="Getting there and around">
    **From Harare:**
    - About 292 km, 3.5 to 4 hours on the A4

    **From Bulawayo:**
    - About 280 km, 4 to 5 hours on the A9 via Zvishavane

    **From Beitbridge:**
    - About 288 km on the A4, the main route from South Africa

    **Getting around:**
    - Taxis are available in town
    - Your own transport is best for Lake Mutirikwi and Mushandike
    - Great Zimbabwe can be reached by taxi or local minibus

    See [travel distances](/essentials/distances).

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="money-bill" title="Money and services">
    - Banks and ATMs in the town centre, but don't rely on them for foreign cards
    - US dollars are widely used. Carry cash for markets and small purchases
    - Several fuel stations. Fill up before heading to Gonarezhou
    - Good mobile coverage in town, patchier in rural areas

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="sun" title="Best time to visit">
    **Year-round destination:** Great Zimbabwe and Masvingo can be visited at any time. Average figures for Masvingo from the World Meteorological Organization (WMO):

    **Dry season (May to October):**
    - Average highs of 22 to 29°C, with cold nights in June and July (around 5 to 6°C)
    - The most comfortable time to explore the ruins

    **Wet season (November to April):**
    - Average highs of 26 to 29°C, with afternoon storms
    - Green landscapes and fuller lakes

    **Gonarezhou:**
    - Some roads become impassable after heavy rain, so the dry season is easiest. Check road conditions with the park before you travel

    See [weather by month](/essentials/weather-by-month).

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## History of Masvingo

<Card>
**Timeline:**

**11th to 15th centuries:**

- Great Zimbabwe grows into the capital of a major trading kingdom
- At its peak it may have housed as many as 18,000 people, though estimates vary widely

**15th century:**

- Great Zimbabwe declines and power shifts to other centres, such as Khami in the west

**1890:**

- The Pioneer Column sets up an encampment, Fort Victoria, around which the town grows

**After independence:**

- The town is renamed Masvingo

**Today:**

- Capital of Masvingo Province
- A university city and the gateway to Great Zimbabwe

**The name:**
"Masvingo" comes from the Shona word for walled enclosures or forts.

</Card>

## Shopping in Masvingo

<Card title="Best buys" icon="bag-shopping">
  **Soapstone carvings:**
  The area is known for soapstone carving, the same stone used for Great Zimbabwe's famous birds.

**Where to buy:**

- Carvers near Great Zimbabwe
- Markets in Masvingo town
- Roadside sellers on the main roads

**Tips:**

- Bargaining is expected, but be fair
- Quality varies, so check the workmanship
- Buying direct supports local artisans

</Card>

## Visitor tips

<Tip>
**Local knowledge:**
- Buy soapstone carvings directly from carvers near Great Zimbabwe
- Great Zimbabwe is much better with a guide
- Lake Mutirikwi is lovely at sunset, so time your visit to the lake accordingly
- If heading to Gonarezhou, stock up on supplies in Masvingo
- Local minibuses to Great Zimbabwe are cheap but irregular, so a taxi is easier
</Tip>

<Warning>
**Important notes:**
- Midday at Great Zimbabwe is very hot from September to March, so start early
- For Gonarezhou, a 4x4 is recommended and you should leave with a full tank
- Malaria risk rises as you head into the lowveld towards Gonarezhou. See a travel health adviser before you go
- Great Zimbabwe closes at 16:30, so don't arrive late
</Warning>
