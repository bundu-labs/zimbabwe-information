**Umfurudzi Park is a 760 km² safari area of granite kopjes, woodland and river frontage in Shamva district, about 130 km by road north-east of Harare.** It is a park in recovery: poaching and hunting emptied it of most big game, and since 2011 it has been restocked with plains game, buffalo, giraffe and elephant. Come for guided walks, game drives, birds and quiet bush within a morning's drive of the capital, not for the Big Five. The dry months from about May to October are the easiest time to visit.

## Umfurudzi at a glance

|                    |                                                                                                             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Size**           | 760 km² (Umfurudzi Park)                                                                                    |
| **Location**       | Shamva district, Mashonaland Central, about 130 km by road north-east of Harare                             |
| **Status**         | A safari area, designated under the Parks and Wildlife Act of 1975                                          |
| **Run by**         | A joint venture between ZimParks and Pioneer Corporation Africa, set up in August 2010                      |
| **Rivers**         | About 82 km of the Mazowe River on the eastern and southern boundary; the Gwetera in the north              |
| **Known for**      | Granite kopjes, river scenery, guided walks, restocked plains game and birds                                |
| **Where to sleep** | Self-catering chalets and tented camps, and campsites                                                       |
| **Fee (2026)**     | US$18 a day for international visitors (US$10 if staying overnight). See [park fees](/essentials/park-fees) |

You will see the park called by three names. The 1975 Act designated it the **Umfurudzi Safari Area**, ZimParks' website lists it as **Umfurudzi Recreational Park**, and the joint venture that runs it calls it **Umfurudzi Park**.

## The landscape

Granite dominates the centre and south of the park, with large kopjes and bare granite domes (dwalas). There are bigger hills in the north-east and flat, open savanna in the north-west. The park describes its vegetation as mopane groves, baobab, combretum and brachystegia (miombo) woodland, and open savanna, with a mix of highveld and lowveld trees along the rivers.

Four main rivers cross or border the park: the **Mazowe, Ruanga, Umfurudzi and Gwetera**. Only the Mazowe and Umfurudzi flow all year. Average rainfall is about 750 mm a year, and nine small dams have been built in the south and centre of the park since 2011.

## Wildlife

Umfurudzi was once a rich game area. The park cites studies by Cambridge University in the 1980s that recorded **more than 100 black rhino, more than 100 elephant, more than 1,500 sable and more than 250 roan**, along with lion, leopard, hyena and cheetah. Heavy poaching followed. ZimParks moved **44 black rhino** out of the park to safer areas, and stopped all hunting in **2007** because animal numbers were so low.

### Restocking since 2011

The joint venture's first game count, in 2010, found very little. The park then began moving animals back in:

- **2011–2012:** 1,410 animals were translocated, including 399 impala, 328 eland, 218 wildebeest, 201 zebra and 163 buffalo.
- **January 2013:** 13 elephants (six adults and seven juveniles) arrived, along with a large male crocodile. A 2023 report says the park had no elephants before 2011.

The park lies in a foot-and-mouth disease-free zone, so new animals could only come from highveld properties.

### What you might see

On its game drives the park names **giraffe, buffalo, zebra and wildebeest**, and antelope herds on walks. A 2023 report by the Zim Morning Post listed **eland, warthog, impala, leopard, buffalo and giraffe** as the park's main animals. Rhino were on the park's 2013–2014 restocking plan, but we couldn't confirm that any have returned. We also couldn't confirm a resident lion population, so don't plan a visit around big cats.

For birders, the park highlights the **African fish eagle** along the rivers and sunbirds in the woodland. See [birding in Zimbabwe](/wildlife/birding-in-zimbabwe).

## Things to do

The park lists these activities:

- **Game drives:** guided drives, usually early morning and late afternoon.
- **Walking safaris:** led by rangers, past kopjes, streams and waterfalls, and rock paintings. Look at rock art without touching it. See [Mashonaland rock art](/rock-art/mashonaland-sites).
- **Bird watching** on quiet trails and along the rivers.
- **Family safaris** and **wilderness camping**.
- **Mountain biking.**

Ask the park office which activities are running and what they cost when you book. Prices are not published.

<Warning>
  Walk only with a park ranger or licensed guide. Buffalo and elephant are back in the park, so don't walk on your own.
</Warning>

## Where to stay

Umfurudzi has three kinds of accommodation, all run by the park:

- **Chalets:** self-catering, with modern amenities.
- **Tented camps:** fixed, self-catering tents.
- **Camping:** bring your own tent and kit. Wilderness camping can be arranged.

All of it is **self-catering**, so bring your food and drinks. The park does not publish the number of units or its rates.

**Bookings:** bookings@umfurudzipark.co.zw, +263 719 687 225 (WhatsApp and calls) or +263 777 005 763 (calls only).

## Getting there

Umfurudzi is an easy drive from Harare, and the road route goes via Shamva.

| From                | Approximate road distance to the park gate | Approximate driving time |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Harare (via Shamva) | 130 km                                     | 2 to 2.5 hours           |
| Bindura             | 70 km                                      | About 1.5 hours          |
| Shamva              | 40 km                                      | About 1 hour             |

Driving via Bindura instead of the direct Shamva road adds about 25 km. Roads in the park are gravel, so ask the park office about conditions before you set off, especially in the rains. See [distances and driving times](/essentials/distances).

<Tip>
  Buy fuel and food in Harare, Bindura or Shamva on the way in. There are no shops in the park.
</Tip>

## Park fees

Umfurudzi has its own line in the ZimParks tariff. **2026 conservation fees for international visitors are US$18 a day (day visit) or US$10 a day if you stay overnight in the park.** SADC visitors pay US$12 or US$6, and Zimbabweans US$6 or US$3.

- **Vehicles (cars, pick-ups and 4x4s):** US$5 a day for a Zimbabwe-registered vehicle and US$10 a day for a foreign-registered vehicle, whether or not you stay overnight.
- **Buses:** US$20 a day if Zimbabwe-registered, US$30 if foreign-registered.

The tariff says prices are pegged in US dollars, and that local visitors may pay in local currency at the prevailing rate. See our [2026 national park fees guide](/essentials/park-fees).

## Best time to visit

- **May to August (cool and dry):** the best time for walking. Days are mild and nights are cold, so pack warm layers.
- **September and October (hot and dry):** the bush is thin and animals stay close to the rivers and dams. October is hot.
- **November to April (rains):** green and good for birds, but gravel roads can be difficult. Check with the park before you go.

Harare's long-term averages are a useful guide to conditions. See [Zimbabwe weather by month](/essentials/weather-by-month) and our [best time for a safari](/wildlife/best-time-for-safari) guide.

## Practical tips

- **Book ahead.** The park is small, and there is no published online booking system.
- **Be self-sufficient.** Bring food, drinking water, a torch and a first-aid kit.
- **Ask about malaria.** Get advice from a travel clinic before you go. See [health and safety](/essentials/health-and-safety).
- **Expect a park in recovery.** The park says the Mazowe River is under pressure from mining, heavy-metal pollution and siltation, and wildlife numbers are still building up. Your fees help pay for the work.
- **Combine it.** Umfurudzi works as a weekend from [Harare](/destinations/harare), or a stop on the way to the north-east.
