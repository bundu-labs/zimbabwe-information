Zimbabwe Travel Information is an independent travel guide to Zimbabwe, written from Zimbabwe. It exists so that people planning a trip here can read about the country from the people who know it, not only from overseas tour sellers and foreign government advisories.

## Who writes it

<Card title="Bryan Fawcett — author and editor" icon="user-pen">
  Bryan writes and edits most of this guide. He is the founder and CEO of Nyuchi Africa, a Zimbabwean technology company, and founder of The Bundu Foundation. He started Zimbabwe Travel Information to give travellers one trustworthy, up-to-date place to plan a Zimbabwe trip, and to make sure local voices are the ones answering their questions.
</Card>

Local contributors also write for the guide: guides, hospitality staff, researchers and students who live and work in Zimbabwe. As contributors' pages are published, they carry the contributor's name.

## How we keep it accurate

- **Practical pages are dated.** Visa rules, fees, currency arrangements and safety advice change often in Zimbabwe. Practical pages show when they were last reviewed and link to the official source.
- **We check official sources.** These include the Department of Immigration's [e-visa portal](https://www.evisa.gov.zw), ZimParks, and the US, UK and Australian travel advisories.
- **We correct mistakes quickly.** If something is wrong or out of date, [tell us](/contact) and we will fix it.

## Become a contributor

If you live in Zimbabwe and know a place, a route or a subject well, you can write for the guide. Contributors apply, submit and track their work through the Nyuchi contributor platform.

<Card title="Apply to contribute" icon="pen-to-square" href="https://platform.nyuchi.com">
  Create an account on platform.nyuchi.com to apply, then submit pages and follow them through review.
</Card>

## How the guide is funded

The guide is free to read and published under a [Creative Commons licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is supported in two ways, and we label both wherever they appear:

- **Bookings.** When you choose to plan a trip through our booking partner, <a href="https://www.moses-adventures.com/?utm_source=travel-info.co.zw&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=about-this-guide" rel="sponsored noopener">Moses Adventures</a>, that referral supports the guide. Booking links are labelled "Booking partner" or appear on our [booking page](/book).
- **The verified business directory.** Lodges, operators and other businesses are listed and verified through [Mukoko Kweli](https://kweli.mukoko.com).

Editorial pages are not paid placements. A business cannot pay to change what we write about a destination.
