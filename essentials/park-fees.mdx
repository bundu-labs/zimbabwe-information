**A day visit to a Zimbabwean national park costs between US$12 and US$58 per person for international visitors in 2026.** The Victoria Falls Rainforest is the most expensive at US$58. Hwange and Mana Pools are US$24 a day. These are the official conservation fees set by the Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks), and they include VAT.

**Written by [Bryan Fawcett](/about-this-guide).**

<Note>
  **Last reviewed: 21 October 2026** against the official ZimParks 2026 tariff, ["De-Gazetted Tariff 2026"](https://www.zimparks.org.zw/media/2026/02/De-Gazetted-Tariff-2026.pdf) (published February 2026). Fees change, usually in January. Check with ZimParks or your lodge before you travel.
</Note>

## Who pays which rate

ZimParks charges three rates:

- **International:** visitors from outside the SADC region, including the UK, US, Europe and Australia.
- **SADC:** citizens of Southern African Development Community countries, such as South Africa, Botswana, Zambia, Namibia and Mozambique. Carry your passport to prove it.
- **Local:** Zimbabwean citizens and residents.

Each rate has two prices. The **day visitor** price applies if you enter and leave the same day. The **staying overnight** price applies to each day you stay inside the park, at a ZimParks camp or a lodge within the park. Lodges often include park fees in their rates, so ask before you pay twice.

### Children

The 2026 tariff does not list a separate child rate for national park conservation fees. Ask at the gate or check with your lodge or operator.

## Vehicle fees (US$ per vehicle per day)

You pay these on top of the per-person fees when you drive yourself into a park.

| Park                                                           | Foreign-registered car, day | Foreign-registered car, overnight | Zimbabwe-registered car, day | Zimbabwe-registered car, overnight |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Hwange, Mana Pools, Zambezi, Matobo, Chizarira, Nyanga, Kariba | 10                          | 3                                 | 5                            | 2                                  |
| Chimanimani, Lake Chivero and other smaller parks              | 10                          | 3                                 | 3                            | 1                                  |
| Matusadona                                                     | 5                           | 5                                 | 2                            | 2                                  |
| Gonarezhou                                                     | 5                           | 2                                 | 5                            | 2                                  |

"Car" covers saloons, pick-ups and 4x4s. A minibus (up to 25 seats) costs US$30 a day if foreign-registered, and a trailer or caravan adds US$10 a day. Overland trucks pay US$100 a day.

## Paying park fees

- **Pay in US dollars.** Bring cash in small, clean notes, because card machines at remote gates are not always working. See our [money guide](/essentials/currency-and-money).
- **Keep your receipt.** Rangers may ask to see it inside the park, and you need it when you leave.
- **Fees went up in 2026.** Most 2026 prices are about 15% higher than in the 2025 tariff (the Rainforest went from US$50 to US$58 for international visitors), and the 2026 tariff states that its prices include VAT. Older guidebooks and websites often still show 2025 prices.
